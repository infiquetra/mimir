# Mimir Strategy

## Target problem

EVE Online players rely on a fragmented ecosystem of outdated, often Windows-only companion tools, forcing them to constantly context-switch across multiple unmaintained apps just to manage their gameplay.

## Our approach

We will build a single, offline-first, cross-platform application that goes beyond basic API pulls to provide real, actionable insights (like actual P&L calculations), so that players can abandon their spreadsheets and fragmented tools.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** EVE Online players navigating the game's extreme complexity - they hire the product to fill in the mechanical gaps (like planning planetary production or analyzing combat) so they can reduce that complexity into actionable plans.

## Key metrics

- **Weekly Active Users (WAU)** - Tracks players returning regularly to manage their gameplay, not just initial downloads; measured via app analytics.
- **Multi-Character Adoption Rate** - The percentage of users linking 2 or more characters; measured via database/analytics.
- **Advanced Feature Usage** - How often players use complex tools (e.g., P&L calculations per week) versus simple wallet checks; measured via event tracking.
- **Crash-Free Sessions / API Success Rate** - Tracks technical health and offline reliability; measured via crash reporting and network logs.

## Tracks

### Core Infrastructure
Building the offline-first database (Drift), the cross-platform UI, and the reliable ESI API integration.
_Why it serves the approach:_ It makes the "offline-first, cross-platform" foundation of the approach possible.

### Actionable Intelligence
Developing complex calculators (like actual P&L), industry tools, and AI-native after-action reports.
_Why it serves the approach:_ It directly serves the goal of pulling players out of their spreadsheets by providing real insights.

### Unified Player Management
Consolidating character tracking, skill queues, wallets, and asset management into a single dashboard.
_Why it serves the approach:_ It fulfills the promise of replacing 5+ fragmented apps with one unified tool.
