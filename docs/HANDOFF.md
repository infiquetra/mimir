## Data model facts you must not re-derive

- `assets/sde/dogma.json` (types, attributes incl. mass=4 and volume=38 from
  invTypes columns, effect ids+names) and
  `assets/sde/effect_modifiers.json` (CCP's resolved `dgmEffects.modifierInfo`:
  func, operator, modified/modifying attribute, domain, skillTypeId, groupId)
  are generated by `scripts/sde/generate_dogma_sde.py` from the fuzzwork dump
  **`csv/` plain-CSV layout** (the old flat `*.csv.bz2` URLs 404). Download
  caches live in `scripts/sde/*.csv` and are gitignored.
- `dgmExpressions` is retired (header-only). Do not plan an expression-tree
  evaluator; the resolved modifiers supersede it.
- Modifier routing in `lib/features/fitting/domain/dogma_engine.dart`:
  - `ItemModifier` + `shipID` → ship attributes (hardeners, damage control).
  - `Location*`/`Owner*` + `shipID` → fitted modules (group-restricted), plus
    the ship when it carries the modified attribute.
  - `charID` → loaded charges AND fitted drones (never fitted modules, or
    damage amps would boost turrets). `ItemModifier` + `charID` + modified 212
    multiplies missile damage components (BCS, pyfa Effect763).
  - Operators: 6 = postPercent, 0 and 4 = postMul. Others counted and ignored
    (operator 2 = missile specialisation family, still queued).
  - `skillTypeID` is a TARGET FILTER (target's required skills 182/183/184).
    Ship-owned bonuses scale with the ship's own required skill
    (`shipSkillLevel`); module-owned bonuses apply raw. pyfa's
    `eos/effects.py` handlers are the authority when data is ambiguous.
- Offense math (pyfa parity): turret volley = charge damage components
  (114/116/117/118) × damage modifier 64; cycle = rate-of-fire 51 (ms);
  launchers contribute charge damage alone; drones: active = in-space or
  fitted count capped by bandwidth (1272/drone), bay usage from volume 38;
  unloaded weapons contribute zero.
- `SdeService` loads `effect_modifiers.json` on EVERY launch (even with a
  seeded DB) and answers `ensureEffectModifiers` bundled-first; Drift cache
  and public ESI are fallbacks only.
